## Remaining dependencies and limits

No live Weebly or editmysite service is needed for the static pages. Weebly-origin CSS, fonts, theme code, and slideshow code remain as local files to preserve appearance and behavior. Legacy names such as `_W`, `Weebly`, and `wsite` in those files are not network dependencies. The broad original service bundles and their references remain only in the excluded archival originals.

Three embedded YouTube videos remain remote: `KbtkpYIbuCw` on Multi-Modal Locomotion, and `wRJO38Mw5sU` and `GymQesLn-WI` on Agility and Manueverability. Their video streams were not downloaded; playback depends on YouTube availability and permission to embed. External article, Google Scholar, university, and other website links remain links. Their current availability was not audited, and their remote content is not preserved by this copy. The standalone site content, navigation, images, fonts, and slideshows work without external access.

A public crawl cannot discover unpublished, password-protected, deleted, or entirely unlinked and unsitemapped pages/files. The crawl exhausted every page and resource found through the homepage, sitemap, and reachable content; it is not a Weebly account export. No forms, account features, or server-backed application workflows were found in the public content. If hidden pages or extra uploads exist in the Weebly editor, they require a separate export/list to preserve.
